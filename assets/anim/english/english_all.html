115 學年度　英文　3B1B 動態講解
The email that you send will ＿＿＿＿ back if the address is incorrect.選出最適合的選項
Atransfervt. 轉移、轉帳
Bgraspvt. 抓住、理解
Cattachvt. 附加、貼上
Dbouncevi. 彈回、退回
完整句：The email that you send will bounce back if the address is incorrect.
ANSWER
(D) bounce
bounce back＝（郵件）被退回
bounce back 是「信件被退回」的固定搭配，看到 back 先想它。
Jenny paused for a moment in ＿＿＿＿ before answering the difficult question.選出最適合的選項
Ahesitationn. 猶豫、遲疑
Bharmonyn. 和諧、融洽
Cleisuren. 閒暇、空閒
Dvolumen. 音量；體積
完整句：Jenny paused for a moment in hesitation before answering the difficult question.
ANSWER
(A) hesitation
pause in hesitation＝遲疑地停頓
pause／reply 前後常接 in hesitation，表示「猶豫地」。
Mr. Lin has some ＿＿＿＿ plans for his new coffee business. More than ten chain stores are scheduled to open next month.選出最適合的選項
Adurableadj. 耐用的
Bambitiousadj. 雄心勃勃的
Cfluentadj. 流利的
Dcircularadj. 圓形的；循環的
完整句：Mr. Lin has some ambitious plans for his new coffee business.
ANSWER
(B) ambitious
ambitious plans＝宏大的計畫
看到大規模擴張、連鎖展店，優先想 ambitious（有野心的）。
Students in this school are encouraged to ＿＿＿＿ the Internet for study purposes in the science class.選出最適合的選項
Adismissvt. 解散；駁回
Baccessvt. 使用、取用
Cencountervt. 遭遇、遇到
Dtargetvt. 以…為目標
完整句：Students are encouraged to access the Internet for study purposes.
ANSWER
(B) access
access the Internet＝連上／使用網路
access 是及物動詞，直接接受詞：access the Internet（不加介系詞）。
Rise of property values in Taiwan has been so ＿＿＿＿ that many people cannot afford housing.選出最適合的選項
Afragileadj. 脆弱的
Bpessimisticadj. 悲觀的
Cdramaticadj. 劇烈的、大幅的
Dfashionableadj. 時尚的
完整句：Rise of property values has been so dramatic that many people cannot afford housing.
ANSWER
(C) dramatic
a dramatic rise＝大幅上漲
dramatic 描述數據變化時是「幅度劇烈」，不是只有戲劇性。
More than 70,000 people have been killed in the Gaza War since 2023. It's a big human ＿＿＿＿.選出最適合的選項
Aprotestn. 抗議
Bsuiciden. 自殺
Ccautionn. 謹慎；警告
Dtragedyn. 悲劇、慘劇
完整句：It's a big human tragedy.
ANSWER
(D) tragedy
a human tragedy＝人類悲劇
大量傷亡的結果，用 tragedy（悲劇）總結。
The U.S. state of Maine experienced severe winter storms ＿＿＿＿ by low temperatures, strong winds, and significant snowfall.選出最適合的選項
Amonitoredvt. 監測、監控
Bcharacterizedbe characterized by 以…為特徵
Cfrustratedadj. 感到挫折的
Djustifiedadj. 被證明為正當的
完整句：Maine experienced severe winter storms characterized by low temperatures, strong winds, and snowfall.
ANSWER
(B) characterized
be characterized by＝以…為特徵
by 後面列出「特徵清單」時，優先想 be characterized by。
Chen Ning Yang, a ＿＿＿＿ researcher in physics, was awarded the Nobel Prize in 1957.選出最適合的選項
Aconsistentadj. 前後一致的
Bdefiniteadj. 明確的
Cprominentadj. 卓越的、著名的
Dgeneticadj. 基因（學）的
完整句：Chen Ning Yang, a prominent researcher in physics, was awarded the Nobel Prize in 1957.
ANSWER
(C) prominent
a prominent researcher＝知名研究者
同位語形容人物時，看後面成就線索：得諾貝爾獎 → prominent。
Suffering from stage ＿＿＿＿, Steve always feels a panic and forgets his lines in front of an audience.選出最適合的選項
Afrightn. 恐懼　stage fright＝怯場
Bpresencen. 風采　stage presence＝舞台魅力
Csuspicionn. 懷疑
Dcrashn. 碰撞、崩潰
完整句：Suffering from stage fright, Steve always feels a panic and forgets his lines.
ANSWER
(A) fright
stage fright＝怯場
後半句說「恐慌、忘詞」，就是怯場 stage fright。
Attracted by the interesting new group project, the students started ＿＿＿＿, but they soon got bored and lost interest.選出最適合的選項
Aapparentlyadv. 顯然地
Benthusiasticallyadv. 熱烈地、興致勃勃地
Cidenticallyadv. 完全相同地
Dmoderatelyadv. 適度地、溫和地
完整句：The students started enthusiastically, but they soon got bored.
ANSWER
(B) enthusiastically
start enthusiastically＝興致高昂地開始
but 前後對比：一開始越熱烈，越能對比後來的無聊。
It's common knowledge that exercise is good for you. ＿＿＿＿ less known is the role that gardening can play in helping to keep you fit and healthy.選出最適合的選項
AThenadv. 然後
BEvenadv. 甚至
CButconj. 但是
DAsconj. 因為；當…時
前句：大家都知道運動有益。
後句（倒裝）：比較少人知道園藝也有益。
完整句：But less known is the role that gardening can play.
ANSWER
(C) But
But less known is…＝但較少人知道的是…
「大家都知道 A」對「較少人知道 B」→ 轉折用 But。
Gardeners might be delighted to learn that the number of calories burned from 30 minutes of gardening is ＿＿＿＿ playing badminton or practicing yoga.選出最適合的選項
Aturned into變成
Bprior to在…之前
Ccounting on依賴、指望
Dcomparable to可與…相比（數量相當）
句型：A is comparable to B＝A 和 B 可相比
完整句：calories burned from 30 minutes of gardening is comparable to playing badminton or practicing yoga.
ANSWER
(D) comparable to
be comparable to＝可與…相比
看到兩個數量被放在一起比，選 comparable to。
…gardeners are more likely to include food produce ＿＿＿＿ their diets.選出最適合的選項
Ain case of以防、萬一
Bon account of因為
Cat risk of有…的風險
Das part of作為…的一部分
句型：include A as part of B＝把 A 納入 B 的一部分
完整句：include food produce as part of their diets.
ANSWER
(D) as part of
as part of their diets＝作為飲食的一部分
include A ______ B，表達「納入、當作一部分」用 as part of。
＿＿＿＿, such as lavenders for mild depression and gingers for headaches, have been embraced by people today.選出最適合的選項
ASomepron. 一些（可數複數）
BBothpron. 兩者都
COtherspron. 另一些（須與 some 對照）
DNonepron. 一個都沒有
線索一：動詞是 have → 主詞必須是複數。
線索二：such as 後面列了薰衣草、薑 → 不只兩項。
完整句：Some, such as lavenders and gingers, have been embraced by people today.
ANSWER
(A) Some
Some …, such as …, have been embraced…
看動詞單複數＋such as 列舉項數，來決定代名詞。
…those who garden every day have wellbeing ＿＿＿＿ 6.6% higher and stress levels 4.2% lower than people who do not garden at all.選出最適合的選項
Ascoresn. 分數、指數
Btriggersn. 觸發因素
Cincreasesn. 增加量
Drecordsn. 紀錄；唱片
句型：have wellbeing scores 6.6% higher＝幸福感分數高出 6.6%
完整句：have wellbeing scores 6.6% higher and stress levels 4.2% lower
ANSWER
(A) scores
wellbeing scores＝幸福感分數
have＋名詞＋比較級形容詞，名詞要能被「高／低」修飾。
From using stars and other natural landmarks to ＿＿＿＿ the position of their ships, to modern technology such as GPS…選出最適合的選項
Acreatevt. 創造
Bdeterminevt. 判定、確定
Cenlargevt. 放大、擴大
Disolatevt. 隔離
句型：use stars to determine the position＝用星星判定船的位置
完整句：using stars and natural landmarks to determine the position of their ships
ANSWER
(B) determine
determine the position＝判定位置
導航的第一步是「知道自己在哪」→ determine the position。
…using the stars, feeling the ＿＿＿＿ of currents, observing seabirds and cloud formation to find new islands.選出最適合的選項
Avisionn. 視力；願景
Bflown. 流動
Clinen. 線；隊伍
Dmatchn. 比賽；相配之物
線索：動詞是 feeling（感覺）＋ of currents（海流）。
完整句：feeling the flow of currents＝感覺海流的流動
ANSWER
(B) flow
the flow of currents＝海流的流動
feel 後面接感官可感的現象：海流的 flow（流動）。
Over time, an insightful view was developed of the natural world, ＿＿＿＿ allowed them to navigate thousands of miles.選出最適合的選項
Athatrel. 那個（不可用於逗號後補述）
Bwhererel. 在那裡（指地方）
Cwhichrel. 這（可指前面整件事）
Dthusadv. 因此
逗號後要能接子句 → 需要關係代名詞。
先行詞是前面「對自然界的洞察」這件事 → 用 which 補述。
完整句：an insightful view was developed of the natural world, which allowed them to navigate thousands of miles.
ANSWER
(C) which
which 補述用法＝可指前面整件事
逗號後補述、且先行詞是整件事 → which，不能用 that。
It was a directional tool that made navigation ＿＿＿＿ even when weather and visibility was poor.選出最適合的選項

Bintensiveadj. 密集的、加強的
Cregularadj. 規律的
Dnoticeableadj. 明顯的

(A) possible
make navigation possible＝使航行變得可行

…allowing sailors to plan their journey and to ＿＿＿＿ their intended route.選出最適合的選項

Bguardvt. 守衛
Ccontributevi. 貢獻（須加 to）
Dvisualizevt. 想像、在心中構思

What is the main idea of the passage?（閱讀測驗・主旨）
A（A）只談個人利益，忽略集體後果非主旨
B（B）共享資源取之不盡，可放心使用與文意相反
C（C）認清個人與集體利益的衝突，是全球合作的關鍵主旨
D（D）國際合作終究無法解決全球議題與文意相反
文章主線：tragedy of the commons＝個人追求自身利益、共用資源被耗盡。
文末轉折：Rather than resigning ourselves… 我們可以探索國際合作的新策略。
主旨＝正視「個人 vs 集體」衝突，才能促成全球合作。
ANSWER
(C)
主旨＝認清個人與集體利益衝突是全球合作關鍵
主旨題抓「全文轉折句」：Rather than resigning… we can explore…。
What does the word “tragedy” in the first paragraph mean?（閱讀測驗・字義）
A（A）a surprising event令人驚訝的事件
B（B）a happy ending快樂結局
C（C）an amusing story有趣的故事
D（D）an event causing great suffering造成重大苦難的事件
第一段描述：共用資源被耗盡、大家一起受害。
tragedy of the commons＝個人自利導致資源枯竭的悲劇。
故 tragedy＝造成重大苦難的事件。
ANSWER
(D)
tragedy＝造成重大苦難的事件
字義題：把字放回原句，用上下文（資源耗盡、共同受害）推義。
What is the author's suggestion in the third paragraph to address the tragedy of the commons?（閱讀測驗・細節）
A（A）各國堅守自身利益優先與作者主張相反
B（B）以更嚴格的法律制裁違規者文中未提及
C（C）探索國際合作的新策略作者建議
D（D）放棄全球減排的目標與作者主張相反
第三段困境：every country wants global emission reductions, but would prefer that someone else take on the burden.
作者建議：explore innovative strategies for international collaboration。
關鍵字：innovative strategies、international collaboration。
ANSWER
(C)
作者建議＝探索國際合作新策略
細節題關鍵字對位：innovative strategies ＋ international collaboration。
Which of the following statements can be inferred from the passage?（閱讀測驗・推論）
A（A）個人過度使用共享資源，會為集體帶來負面後果可由文章推得
B（B）個人自利行為最終只傷害自己一個人與文意不符
C（C）共享資源的問題只會發生在漁業過度窄化
D（D）國際合作對環境議題沒有幫助與文意相反
文中推論基礎：when two individuals act in their own self-interest, they would both experience worse outcomes.
推論：個人過度使用共用資源 → 集體承受負面後果。
ANSWER
(A)
可推得＝個人過度使用共享資源傷害集體
推論題：選可由原文「合理推得」的選項，不選文中沒說的。
What does “their” in the third paragraph refer to?（閱讀測驗・指涉）
A（A）individuals個人們
